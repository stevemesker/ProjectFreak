The Hub is a scene free of combat where players have access to various mechanics and utilities they'll need to overcome dungeons. It is areas built for cooling down and experiences more of the narrative of the game.

# [[Black Lantern Bar]]
This is the first Hub area in the game. It is an old bar run by [[Mama]] that doubles as an operations base for [[The Black Lantern]]s. The bar is eventually destroyed when caught in a conflict between the invading [[Fiend]]s and the [[Lumina Guard]], causing [[Hazen]] and some of the crew to fall through the [[Pallium]] into [[Cradle]]

The bar has some basic amenities like access to basic items and reagents as well as [[Hazen]]'s lab where players can craft basic runes and items. Players can discover more npc's to join the bar that offer new tiers of items and other capabilities

# [[Cradle]]
This is the final hub area of the game. It is a small town that attached itself to the [[Pallium]] walls to protect its inhabitants from the [[Fiend]]ish hoards that have taken over the world.

[[Hazen]] reconstructs his lab and makes some upgrades to allow easier/better rune and item synthesization and new shops are unlocked here.
