The Rune Field

The rune field is a ui space composed of the field itself, the [[Core Node]], and [[Ability Node]]s. Players will grab [[Element Rune]]s from the [[inventory]] window on the right and drag them onto the rune field. When the [[Element Rune]] is placed close enough to the [[Core Node]] a [[Bridge]] will connect the two nodes together, powering the [[Element Rune]] and adding its stat boosts to the [[Shade]] in the currently selected [[Shade Slot]].

Placing an [[Element Rune]] near another [[Element Rune]] will create a [[Bridge]] between them and sending any power one is receiving is sent to the next. This creates a chain of powered [[Element Rune]]s that will branch out to unlock abilities farther out via the [[Ability Node]]s. 

When a [[Bridge]] is created between an [[Element Rune]] and anything else, it becomes tethered and is only allowed to move within range of all of its other currently tethered [[Element Rune]]s. If the player pulls hard enough on the tethered rune it will eventually snap, thus breaking the connection and possibly severing power access from the [[Core Node]].

When a powered [[Element Rune]] overlaps am [[Ability Node]] it can potentially activate the node, giving the [[Shade]] a significant ability or upgrade. All [[Ability Node]]s are predefined and the same for every single [[Shade Slot]].
