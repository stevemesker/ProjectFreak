The fail state of the [[Dungeon]] occurs when [[Hazen]]'s [[Health]] drops to <= 0 or his shade dies and runs out of [[LIFE]] pointes. This can only really happen in dungeons as there are no enemies that can damage [[Hazen]] outside of a [[Dungeon]]. When either of those events occur, [[Hazen]] casts a portal back to his safe house and xp/loot is distributed based on how well the player did.
