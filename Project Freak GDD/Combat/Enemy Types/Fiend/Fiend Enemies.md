[[Fiend]]s are other worldy entities that have made their way into [[Pallium]]. They tend to be very bestial and lack any real strategy or tactic beyond slaughtering anything that moves

---
[[Popcorn]]
[[Scarabs]] - small bug creatures that move in large herds created by the [[Watcher Fiend]]. They do not have any direct abilities and only use basic attacks. They heavily rely on swarming their prey with large numbers

Basic Attack - a very short thrust attack
Abilities - none

[[Burst Scarabs]] - a variant of their lesser cousins, these enemies operate in the same way as the [[Scarabs]] however instead of physically attacking they explode leaving behind a small toxic patch on the ground that does magic damage for a short time

Basic Attack:
a very short thrust attack

Abilities:
Detonate - Spawns a poison patch on the Burst Scarab's location and destroys itself

[[Lurker]] - four legged beasts that specialize in damage. They are close range melee attackers that lack any high level reasoning for speed and tactics.

Basic attack:
Two fairly narrow swiping swings. No combo effects

Abilities:
Lunge - a short wind up before dashing a short distance forward, stopping early if they hit an enemy. Only used to close long ranges

Speed
[[Locust]] - flying enemies that specialize in speed. They tend to stay far away from enemies and lob magical ranged attacks very slowly

Basic Attack:
a short thrusting melee attack that can pierce through multiple enemies. Single hit, no combo

Abilities:
Sap Grenade - [[Locust]] charges up and lobs a ball of goo that hit the floor and spawns a small patch that slows enemies

Stinger Missile - [[Locust]] fires a small projectile at the target that move in a straight line. Short cooldown

[[Living Hive]] - An immobile mound of eggs that spawn up to 10 [[Scarabs]] or [[Burst Scarabs]] until destroyed.

Basic Attack: None

Abilities:
Hatch scarab - An egg rolls out of the hive that bursts into either  [[Burst Scarabs]] or [[Scarabs]]. Can only have up to a certain amount on the field. The scarabs will eventually despawn if no enemies can be found after a short time

Strength Speed
[[Chaos Knight]] - A fiend from the [[Dark King]]'s own military. They make up the majority of the fiendish soldiers on the frontlines of humanities extinction. [[Chaos Knight]]s come in a melee and a ranged variant

Basic Attack:
Melee Variant - Two hit combo attack. Wide swing and then forward thrust
Ranged Variant - Single projectile attack

Melee Variant Abilities:
Chaos Dive - Dash attack where the knight does a short charge up and leaps into the air. He crashes down at a distance in front of him. Targets directly under his landing attack take massive damage and are knocked back. The knight then takes some time to recover before moving again

Ranged Variant Abilities:
Sharp Shooter - Ranged attack where the knight charges and fires an arrow. It slowly moves forward and if a hostile creature crosses in front it will track the target. It has a very slow turning radius so avoiding it is possible

Fiendish Archer - Fires 3 projectiles in quick succession

[[Stalkers]] - an evolved creature that takes aspects from both [[Lurker]]s and [[Locust]]s. It can use short bursts of flight to quickly enter and exit fights and deal massive damage with its huge sword appendages. It also has the unique trait of turning invisible when activated

Basic Attack:
3 hit melee combo. Twi swipes and a thrust

Abilities:
Stalker Cloaking - The Stalker turns invisible for a short period of time. They'll attempt to reposition themselves once cloaked for a surprise attack

Stalker Backstab - Requires the [[Stalkers]] to be cloaked. The cloak drops and the fiend charges up their attack, leaving them open for a second. It then lunges forward a short distance in a massive attack

Stalker Flight - the fiend jumps high into the air and lands in an area within a range of their takeoff point. Can be used in or out of cloak.

Speed Defense
[[Juggernaut]] - A massive creature with protective plates hammered into its hide. It charges up and rushes forward, destroying enemies and buildings in its wake

Basic Attack:
Two hit melee combo. Slash and smash hit

Abilities:
Roar of Chaos - when low on health the fiend automatically applies Roar of chaos buff to itself. Its attack speed improves and many of its attacks leave behind chaotic flames

Immovable Chaos - The fiend charges up for a long time before charging forward with a very low turning speed. Can destroy buildings and knocks back any creatures it hits. If Roar of chaos is applied it leaves behind a trail of hell fire for a short time
