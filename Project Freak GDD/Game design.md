**Overview**

Synopses: Project freak is an action tamer game where the user plays as [[Hazen]], a [[Shadow Tamer]], who imbues artifacts with his shadow to use mystical gadgets and, more importantly, command their [[Shade]] to help them survive dungeons.

---

**Core Loop**

Players will take their collected materials to synthesize items and [[Dive]] into a [[Dungeon]]. Every dungeon is randomly generated and gets longer and more difficult as the game progresses. The dungeon consists of large winding corridors randomly generated that connect to large poi rooms that can have loot, monster fights, and boss encounters. If the player can successfully make it to the end of that chapter’s dungeon they receive rewards and advance the story. If they die in the dungeon they are returned to the last safe area with maybe small repercussions. If their Shade was killed it will lose one of its lives and the player will have to decide if they want to risk running with it again or [[synthesize]] it into materials for their next beast.
