[[DIS]] - Discipline. How likely the shade is to follow orders. May also decide how many shade spells the player can cast through it
[[WILD]] - Wildness. How likely the shade is to go berserk and attack things instead of following its orders

These are the obedience cost for raw power. Attack-heavy [[Element Rune]]s tend to raise WILD and lower DIS. A wild shade can mow down enemies, but it might also leave the team, ignore orders, or walk into traps it would normally avoid. This ties into the berserk personality in the [[Unit Brain]] and balances attack-heavy builds outside the damage math (see [[Damage Balance]]).
