These are the primary tools of a [[Shadow Tamer]] that allow them to modify their shades via the [[Rune Field]]. [[Rune Field Access Point]]s are set up in various locations by [[Keepers]] to allow them to modify their [[Shade]].

# Access Point Types
There are two types of [[Rune Field Access Point]]s. Advanced and basic.

Advanced gives access to all mechanics of the [[Rune Field]] and its various tools:

-Rune Field Editing: Adding, removing, and changing runes in the [[Rune Field]]
-Shade slot changing: The player can not only change the runes of their active shade slot but all of them

Basic is a far more limited type that is typically located within dungeon floors:
-Rune Field Editing

*Note: More mechanics may be added to this list later as the game progresses*
# Access Point Locations
### Hub

The [[Hub Scene]]s contain the most advanced [[Rune Field Access Point]] built by [[Hazen]]. These areas are intended to be a safe prep area for the player to plan out strategies. The first hub zone located in [[The Black Lantern Bar]] and the second is in [[Laughing Man Inn]] in [[Cradle]]. 

### Dungeon Entrance/Boss Floor

Dungeons will always start and end with a basic [[Rune Field Access Point]]. The entrance version is designed as a catch in case the player forgot to tune something or realized the dungeon map layout will take them on a different course than they initially prepared for.

The Boss floor always has a small area before entering the actual boss fight. This is the player's last chance to add any new runes they've picked up or change their strategy.

### Safe Floors

[[Safe Floor]]s and [[Vault Floor]]s will usually have a basic [[Rune Field Access Point]] though they don't have to. It won't be a hard rule but a general level design goal to include them. These floors usually have some sort of good loot in them and we want the player to be able to use them right away if they desire.
