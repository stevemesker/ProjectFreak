Right hand to the [[Quiet King]] and speaker of the [[Lumina Council]]. He is the eldest member of the council and one of the last few remaining [[Shadow Tamer]]s. When [[Stolas]] first arrived, he needed a powerful [[Shadow Tamer]] and thought at first to use [[Lord Old Man]] until discovering [[Hazen]]. This was very fortunate for the dark prince as [[Lord Old Man]] was on to his scheme.
