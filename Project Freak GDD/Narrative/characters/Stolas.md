A member of the [[Lumina Council]] that kicks off the story by hiring [[Hazen]]. He is secretly the [[Dark Prince Stolas]] who seeks to unleash the light of [[Pallium]] for his own mysterious purposes. he is always accompanied by his associate [[Lady Kimaris]].

---
**Stolas's plan**
As the [[Demon Prince]] of knowledge, Stolas heard stories of a hidden city created to grow a powerful artifact that could kill the [[Dark King]]. These stories eventually lead him to the location of [[Pallium]] where he and his second in command [[Lady Kimaris]] eventually managed to infiltrate. Within the great sphere was a massive sun made of raw divine power. He knew exactly what its source is and it was exactly what he needed for his plans to overthrow the [[Dark King]]. He positioned himself within a noble family and eventually rose to the ranks of the [[Lumina Council]]. This new station got him close enough to the [[power source]] of [[Pallium]] but it was locked too securely by the [[Keepers]] magics. Apparently only one of their [[Shadow Tamer]]s could unlock it but they were wiped out long before Stolas arrived. The only remaining [[Shadow Tamer]] was [[Lord Kestrel]] and the last [[Keepers]] was the [[Quiet King]] himself. Neither man would be willing to unlock the [[power source]] and Stolas wasn't sure what to do... until [[Lady Kimaris]] found [[Hazen]]...
