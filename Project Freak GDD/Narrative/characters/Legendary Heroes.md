Long ago the great [[Sage]]s peered into the future and saw a great evil coming to their world. It threatened to bring a [[Cataclysm]] that would sunder the world of [[Alestria]] and bring an end to mankind. But in their visions they saw 4 lights, brighter than all others that would defeat this great evil and usher in a new age of peace. These lights were dubbed the [[Legendary Heroes]]

[[The Summoner]] whose wisdom will call them
[[The Mage]] whose ambition will empower them
[[The Druid]] whose fury will bind them
[[The Paladin]] whose light will guide them
