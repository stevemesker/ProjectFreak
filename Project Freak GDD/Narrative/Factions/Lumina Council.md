Ruling government body of [[Sanctoria]]. They worship the light at the center of the city and claim absolute rulership over Sanctoria and anything the light touches. The head of the Lumina is the [[Silent King]] who is one of the last Scholar [[Keepers]] in the city. He is the only one who can read the city layout and knows the city is actually a powerful weapon that must be kept safe by any means necessary.
