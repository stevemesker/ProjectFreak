A group of scholars who hold the old stories from before [[Sanctoria]] was built. They were hunted by the [[Lumina Council]] in order to keep the people from wanting to leave their safe city. Keepers are classified as [[Orators]] or [[Scholars]]. Orators keep their history by memorizing and speaking their specific knowledge until their assigned scholar can write it down in great detail. Scholars use special codes to etch in the stories told by the Orators.
