A group of scholars who hold the old stories from before [[Sanctoria]] was built. They were hunted by the [[Lumina Council]] in order to keep the people from wanting to leave their safe city. Keepers are classified as [[Orator]]s or [[Scholar]]s. [[Orator]]s keep their history by memorizing and speaking their specific knowledge until their assigned [[Scholar]] can write it down in great detail. [[Scholar]]s use special codes to etch in the stories told by the [[Orator]]s.
