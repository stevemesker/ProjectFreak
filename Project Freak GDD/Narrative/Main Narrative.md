**Synopsis**
[[Project Freak]] is meant to be an intro game to the greater world of [[Alestria]] which is the setting of several games planned. It is a story of how people process great tragedies and how fear blinds us.

Functionally, [[Project Freak]] acts as a single point for the players to learn the world and expands out, literally. By coming in to a broken world that must be repaired, every story expands out from the center seed called the [[Pallium]].

---
**Lore**
Before the games take place, sages of old predicted the rise of the greatest being of evil called the [[Dark King]]. They saw that the only way to defeat this great evil was through the combined might of 4 [[Legendary Heroes]]: [[The Summoner]], [[The Mage]], [[The Druid]], and [[The Paladin]]. The heroes were selected from the greatest men and women from the most powerful kingdoms. On the day of the [[Dark King]]'s arrival the [[Legendary Heroes]] were there, ready to slay him. However [[The Druid]] betrayed the group, allowing the [[Dark King]] to take over [[Alestria]] in a great [[Cataclysm]] that sundered the planet.

One kingdom did not trust the visions of the sages and their Heroes, so they devised a secret plan. They created an artifact that would grow into an unstoppable power of light that no [[Fiend]] could stop. However, they knew such a power would take too long to fully mature so they sealed it, along with their people, in a massive seed called the [[Pallium]]. They believed the [[Dark King]] would eventually succeed and usher in an age of darkness. But they also believed that in time, the [[Dark King]] would become complacent and his armies lazy. When the [[Dark King]] finally thought he could rest, the [[Pallium]] would hatch and the divine light of their weapon will crush his army of [[Fiend]]s and bring on a new age of light.

Centuries have passed, the world is a shattered wasteland, and the [[Pallium]] still sleeps...

Chapter 1 *The premise* | "The Last Keeper"
*Goal*: Tutorial Section. Establish premise and lore

[[Hazen]] fights a [[Fiend]] from [[Outworld]] on his way to [[The Black Lantern]]. [[Lady Kimaris]] was lurking near by to investigate rumors of another living [[Shadow Tamer]] . [[Hazen]] summons his [[Shade]], confirming her suspicions. Later, [[Lady Kimaris]] approaches [[Mama]] with a job to hire [[The Black Lantern]]s as investigators to look into [[Fiend]]s breaching into [[Sanctoria]], knowing [[Hazen]] is the only one who could deal with something this dangerous.

Dungeon: [[Sanctoria Slums]]
[[Hazen]] believes the hole is likely somewhere deep in lower [[Sanctoria]]'s slums. When he arrives, the people are gone and [[Fiend]]s have taken over. Eventually he finds an unusual structure with the source of the demonic power, a massive pyramid that is built with [[Lumina Council]] technology that is keeping a massive hole in the walls of [[Pallium]] open.

Boss: [[Watcher Fiend]]

With the [[Watcher Fiend]] defeated and the hole sealed [[Hazen]] meets with his contact [[Lady Kimaris]]. She confirms his suspicions that someone linked to the council is involved. Though she's not exactly sure who but her benefactor thinks it is linked to the extermination of the [[Keepers]] and the great secret of [[Pallium]]. She requests that [[Hazen]] continue to help her investigate as he might be the only person her boss can trust. [[Hazen]] agrees.
