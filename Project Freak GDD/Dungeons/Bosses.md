[[Watcher Fiend]] 
A massive fiend summoned by [[Stolas]] to punch a hole in [[Pallium]] to allow [[Fiend]]s in to cause chaos. This boss keeps that hole opened and guards it, but there are many of them spread out among the small towns of [[Lower Sanctoria]].

[[Watcher Fiend]] is a massive stationary creature that uses minions it calls from its back while snaring the player in slowing traps. The base of its body is an egg sack, every time the player lowers the boss's health by a certain percentage (to be determined during play tests) it releases an egg that creates a lieutenant level enemy.

Once all eggs are destroyed from its body, the fiend enters the final phase where it crawls out of the hole and attacks the player directly by grabbing and throwing them

[[Lumina Dreadnaught]]
One of the [[Lumina Council]]'s more devastating weapons sent to combat the rising fiendish threat. The council has decided to lock down [[Lower Sanctoria]] and has authorized its soldier to use overwhelming force in order to protect the more powerful nobles of [[Upper Sanctoria]]

the first phase of the battle is fighting [[General Izen]] who was the soldier responsible for running the operation to wipe out the [[Keepers]]. He is a slow but strong brawler enemy, slightly larger than the player. He uses a massive gauntlet that allows him to dash punch and stun the player. He also carries a massive blunderbuss allowing him to attack at medium ranges.

The [[Lumina Dreadnaught]] is called in by [[General Izen]]  when his health bar reaches 50%. The general escapes in the explosion that starts phase 2. The [[Lumina Dreadnaught]] is a large slow moving mech that fires rockets and a massive radiant beam canon. Players will need to hide behind cover and sneak behind to find its weak spot that breaks its armor, opening the boss to the final phase.

Without its armor, the [[Lumina Dreadnaught]] can no longer use its radiant canon and now switches to a machine gun and firing rockets in random directions. Without the weight of the armor, the boss becomes significantly faster but has way less defense. The third phase should feel frantic and chaotic as the player needs to focus on damaging the dreadnaught before it manages to deliver a killing blow.

[[Lord Kestrel]]
Lord Kestrel starts out as a stealthy assassin who uses a spear and shadow magic to hide himself and strike behind the player. 

When brought down to 75% he summons his shade, the reaper, and switches to firing magical lances while the reaper continues to hide and strike when the player isn't looking.

Once the reaper or [[Lord Kestrel]] is brought down to 50%, he fuses with his shade and becomes the reaper. He uses a massive scythe to do wide slashing attacks and continues to summon magical lances

Once defeated and the player returns to this boss fight, they will only fight the reaper who uses similar move sets as [[Lord Kestrel]] but does not have the sequence of the fusion.
