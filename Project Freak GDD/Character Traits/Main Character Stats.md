**Where Hazen's combat stats come from**
Hazen's level is his [[Tamer Level]], which only unlocks tools and never raises stats. His combat stats ([[Character Stats]]) come from [[Element Rune]]s: a rune can give stats to the shade, to Hazen, or both, sometimes trading one for the other (for example −1 STR to the shade, +2 STR to Hazen). That way Hazen grows with the same rune progression as his shades and doesn't fall behind deeper enemies.
