The shade is released from its tether and becomes a full unit in the level. It can take damage, die, and make its own decisions like navigating and using abilities, depending on its [[DIS]] and [[WILD]].

- If the shade is [[Tethered]], it leaves the tether and is released instead
- If it's already released, using this again puts it away
- Entering a dungeon returns a released shade, and it doesn't follow Hazen to the next floor
- Required for [[Control Shade]]
- Unlocked at [[Tamer Level]] 2

*Undecided:* whether a released shade can only stay out for a limited amount of time (from the old Separate Shade note).
