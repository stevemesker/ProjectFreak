Bring the shade out from your shadow in its [[Tethered]] form. It stays tethered to Hazen, floating beside him, but otherwise acts independently depending on the shade's [[DIS]] and [[WILD]].

- If the shade is [[Release Shade|released]], it comes back and is tethered instead
- If it's already tethered, using this again puts it away
- Unlocked at [[Tamer Level]] 1

See [[Shade]] (Summoning Styles) and [[Shade Forms Plan]].
