All characters have to navigate the [[Dungeon]] via some sort of movement. The different types of movement are as follows:


---
**Movement Types**

| Type  | Description                                                                                                                           |
| :---- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Walk  | Walks around of their legs. Can maneuver over short [[obstacles]] but will activate floor traps and cannot cross over tall obstacles. |
| Fly   | Flies over short and tall [[obstacles]] but cannot fly indoors                                                                        |
| Float | floats over small [[obstacles]] but can activate floor traps. Can turn [[noncorporeal]] for a limited time                            |
| Swim  | can go under short [[obstacles]] and can temporarily go underwater to avoid being attacked                                            |

---
**Movement types explained**
Walk - the simplest type of movement that 90% of all units will use, such as the [[Main Character]]. Walking units can move inside and outside structures and can operate basic interactable objects. They can pick up items on the ground and it offers no real immunity.

Fly - Flying units can hover suspended in the air. They can travel over [[obstacles]] without fear of activating floor traps. They can be immune to certain low attacks however flying creatures cannot go into buildings without landing. They can however fly on top of buildings and other hard to reach areas. Because of this limitation, fliers can usually land and become walking units

Float - Similar to flying, floating units hover over the ground and are not effected by short [[obstacles]] however they can still trigger floor traps. Floating characters can go indoors but cannot reach elevated areas. Some floating units have the ability to turn [[noncorporeal]], allowing them to pass through walls and obstacles and avoid taking damage. However the time in this form is limited and they cannot interact with anything as well.

Swim - Units with this property can hide under the floor level, allowing them to go under locked doors and avoid traps when diving under. Like float's [[noncorporeal]], this is a limited amount of time the swimmer can dive.

---
**Movement and Turning**
Regardless of walk types, movement and turning should work the same for the player. Left stick/WASD control the player's move direction based on the angle of the camera. So no matter what the camera is doing, hitting left will make the character move left on their screen.

Turning is where it gets more complicated. Though the player will always move laterally with the camera how they face can change. When the player is not pressing right stick or moving their mouse the character will face their direction of movement. When the aiming controls are activated the player will face that direction (relative to the camera as with movement). This means that moving left while facing right should look like the character is walking in reverse. Once the turning controls have not been used for a few seconds the players rotation will once again be based on their movement direction.
